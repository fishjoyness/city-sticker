## Landmark anchors

1. 成都大熊猫繁育研究基地 (`chengdu-panda-base`): black-and-white giant panda; layered bamboo; recognizable red-roofed modern panda pavilion; green research-park setting.
2. 武侯祠 (`wuhou-shrine`): red enclosure walls; dark traditional roofs; ceremonial gate; cypress-lined approach.
3. 锦里 (`jinli-street`): dense traditional shopfronts; red lantern canopy; narrow pedestrian lane; Sichuan timber façades.
4. 宽窄巷子 (`kuanzhai-alley`): grey-brick courtyard gates; broad flagstone alley; carved timber doors; restrained teahouse street life.
5. 杜甫草堂 (`du-fu-thatched-cottage`): low thatched-roof cottage; bamboo and old trees; stream/stone bridge; scholarly garden calm.
6. 文殊院 (`wenshu-monastery`): yellow monastery walls; layered dark roofs; symmetrical gate/courtyard; respectful Buddhist-site depiction.
7. 青羊宫 (`qingyang-palace`): dark-roofed Taoist hall; red columns; distinctive bronze goat sculpture; shaded courtyard.
8. 金沙遗址博物馆 (`jinsha-site-museum`): broad curved modern museum roof; glass façade; archaeological park landscaping; sun-bird motif only as restrained architectural cue.
9. 安顺廊桥 (`anshun-bridge`): covered bridge with ornate dark roofs; multiple warm-lit arches; Jin River reflections; riverside city context.
10. 东郊记忆 (`eastern-suburb-memory`): red-brick industrial factory; tall smokestack; exposed truss/pipe details; repurposed arts district.
11. 春熙路 (`chunxi-road`): modern pedestrian shopping street; IFS climbing panda façade cue; dense contemporary storefront canyon; no invented logos/text.
12. 成都人民公园 (`peoples-park-chengdu`): Heming Teahouse pavilion; bamboo chairs and low tables; pond and mature trees; relaxed public-park composition.
13. 望江楼公园 (`wangjiang-tower-park`): four-storey Wangjiang/Chongli Pavilion; green glazed roofs; red columns; bamboo grove by Jin River.
14. 都江堰 (`dujiangyan`): Yuzui fish-mouth levee; splitting river channels; suspension bridge; mountain foothills and water-engineering geometry.
15. 青城山 (`qingcheng-mountain`): forested mountain steps; red-pillared Jianfu/temple gate; layered dark roofs; dense misty evergreen setting.

## Differentiation notes

- Religious and historical compounds are separated through wall color, gate form, distinctive bronze-goat anchor, thatched cottage, and tower silhouette.
- Urban nightlife, shopping, industrial heritage, and covered bridge assets use different dominant structures.
- No generated text survives into formal assets; Chinese nameplates are deterministic.
