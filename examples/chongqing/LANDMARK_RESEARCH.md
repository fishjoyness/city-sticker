## Landmark anchors

1. 洪崖洞 (`hongya-cave`): dense cliffside stilt-house tiers; warm night windows; many stacked eaves; river and bridge context.
2. 解放碑 (`jiefangbei`): tall pale clock monument; stepped pedestal; surrounding dense commercial towers; pedestrian plaza.
3. 长江索道 (`yangtze-river-cableway`): red cable car; twin cables; broad Yangtze; steep layered skyline on both banks.
4. 李子坝轻轨穿楼 (`liziba-monorail`): monorail train visibly passing through residential tower opening; steep road/river urban context.
5. 朝天门 (`chaotianmen`): wedge-shaped river confluence; broad steps/plaza; Yangtze and Jialing waters; ship traffic.
6. 重庆来福士 (`raffles-city-chongqing`): multiple sail-like towers; horizontal sky conservatory; river-confluence podium.
7. 重庆人民大礼堂 (`great-hall-of-the-people-chongqing`): large circular blue-glazed roof; red body; broad stone podium; symmetrical wings.
8. 重庆中国三峡博物馆 (`three-gorges-museum`): broad curved dome; large glass façade; monumental steps; museum plaza.
9. 磁器口古镇 (`ciqikou`): steep stone lane; dense dark timber façades; red lanterns; layered hillside roofs.
10. 山城步道 (`mountain-city-trail`): steep stairway; cliffside railings; old brick houses; layered high-rise skyline below.
11. 白象居 (`baixiangju`): dense connected residential blocks; open-air sky bridges/corridors; dramatic vertical hillside levels.
12. 鹅岭二厂 (`eling-testbed-2`): industrial concrete/brick buildings; rooftop terrace; old print-factory details; hillside skyline overlook.
13. 南山一棵树 (`nanshan-one-tree`): circular/arched observation deck; one prominent old tree; panoramic peninsula skyline across river.
14. 武隆天生三桥 (`wulong-three-natural-bridges`): monumental karst natural stone arch; deep green gorge; narrow path and traditional inn below for scale.
15. 大足石刻 (`dazu-rock-carvings`): Baodingshan rock-carving wall; dense Song-era relief figures; cliff niche architecture; respectful non-cute treatment.
