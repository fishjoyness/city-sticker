# Qingdao Landmark Research

Research basis: the supplied V1 candidate list, with anchors centered on Qingdao's German-era waterfront, red-roof historic districts, coastal parks, beer heritage and Laoshan landscape. Religious and memorial sites remain architectural and restrained.

## Locked anchors

栈桥—long stone pier, white pavilion, open bay; 八大关—tree-lined villas and red roofs; 五四广场—open waterfront civic plaza with the red May Wind sculpture as its visual anchor; 青岛啤酒博物馆—historic red-brick brewery and beer motifs; 圣弥厄尔教堂—twin-spire stone church; 青岛基督教堂—yellow stone church and hill; 小青岛—white lighthouse and rocky islet; 信号山—red mushroom lookout roofs and hill; 崂山—granite peaks, pines and sea; 石老人—sea stack and beach; 奥帆中心—sails and marina; 小麦岛—curving green coast; 德国总督楼—historic red-roof villa; 海底世界—coastal aquarium entrance; 青岛火车站—red-roof station and clock tower.
