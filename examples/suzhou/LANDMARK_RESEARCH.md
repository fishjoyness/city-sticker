## Landmark anchors

1. 拙政园 (`humble-administrators-garden`): broad lotus pond; zigzag waterside corridor; white walls and black-tile pavilions; layered rockery and planting.
2. 留园 (`lingering-garden`): compact courtyards; covered corridor; pale Taihu scholar rock; white wall and dark roof framing.
3. 狮子林 (`lion-grove-garden`): dense labyrinthine Taihu rockery; stone openings; pond edge; traditional pavilion behind.
4. 虎丘 (`tiger-hill`): visibly leaning Yunyan Pagoda; wooded hill; stone approach; warm brick tower body.
5. 寒山寺 (`hanshan-temple`): yellow temple walls; dark upturned roofs; bell tower/pagoda silhouette; canal-side bridge context.
6. 苏州博物馆 (`suzhou-museum`): I. M. Pei geometric white walls; charcoal polygon roofs; central water court; angular stone-and-bamboo composition.
7. 平江路 (`pingjiang-road`): narrow canal; whitewashed houses with black roofs; low stone bridge; waterside lane and boat.
8. 山塘街 (`shantang-street`): canal street; red lanterns; white-and-black Jiangnan shopfronts; arched bridge and boat.
9. 盘门 (`panmen-gate`): fortified water-and-land gate; grey masonry walls; gate tower; canal opening and Wu Gate Bridge context.
10. 网师园 (`master-of-nets-garden`): intimate central pond; waterside pavilion; compact rockery; enclosed white-wall courtyard.
11. 沧浪亭 (`canglang-pavilion`): square pavilion with dark sweeping roof; stone terrace; bamboo and old trees; winding water outside garden wall.
12. 金鸡湖 (`jinji-lake`): broad lake; Suzhou skyline; Gate of the Orient; lakeside promenade and modern city reflection.
13. 东方之门 (`gate-of-the-orient`): monumental trouser-shaped arch skyscraper; twin vertical legs; curved joining crown; modern plaza base.
14. 周庄古镇 (`zhouzhuang`): twin bridges over converging canals; dense whitewashed waterside houses; black-tile roofs; wooden boat.
15. 同里古镇 (`tongli`): three-bridge water-town motif; stone arch bridge; quieter canal; white walls, black roofs, willow and boat.

## Differentiation notes

- The six classical gardens use distinct dominant silhouettes: broad pond, corridor-and-rock, rock labyrinth, intimate pond, pavilion-and-bamboo, and museum geometry.
- Pingjiang, Shantang, Zhouzhuang, and Tongli are separated by street density, lantern treatment, twin-bridge composition, and three-bridge/quiet-canal composition.
