# Wuhan Landmark Research

Research basis: Wuhan municipal government and Wuhan culture/tourism sources. The set emphasizes the city's verified Jiangcheng identity: landmark towers, Yangtze bridges, East Lake, Chu culture museums, riverside historic buildings and traditional religious/heritage sites.

The Wuhan Yangtze Bridge is documented by the municipal government as a double-deck road-rail bridge and major Wuhan landmark; Qingchuan Pavilion is described as a riverside historic architectural complex. The Hubei Museum source confirms its Chu-culture and Marquis Yi bronze-bell anchors. Historical memorial imagery remains architectural and restrained, without entertainment styling.
